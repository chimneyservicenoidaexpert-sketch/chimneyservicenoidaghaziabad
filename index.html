Chimney Service - 15 Years Expert Guide

With 15 years experience in Noida Ghaziabad, we know when, why and how chimney fails.

When Should You Get Service?

Every 3-4 months normal cooking, every 2 months heavy frying. Signs: low suction, oil dripping, heavy noise.

Why Does Chimney Stop Working?

Oil choking in blower, PCB failure due to moisture, touch sensor water damage, motor bearing dry due to heat and voltage fluctuation.

Why Does Chimney Make Noise?

Dry bearing grinding sound, unbalanced blower due to oil deposit one side, loose wall clamp vibration, small bent duct air pressure noise. Fixed with high temperature grease.

Why Does Suction Become Low?

Filter 70% blocked, auto clean only removes 40% oil. Our chemical dip removes 100% oil and suction becomes like new.
