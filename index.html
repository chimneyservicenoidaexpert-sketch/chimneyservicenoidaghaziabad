When Should You Get Chimney Service Done?

For normal cooking 2 times daily, service every 3-4 months. For heavy oil, frying daily, service every 2 months. Signs are low suction, oil dripping, heavy noise, auto clean not working. Regular service increases motor life from 2 to 6 years.

Why Does Chimney Stop Working?

Main reason is oil choking inside blower housing. Oil becomes hard carbon and blocks air flow. Second is PCB failure due to moisture and voltage fluctuation. Touch panel fails when water goes inside. Motor bearing gets dry due to heat.

Why Does Chimney Make Noise?

Noise from four reasons. First dry bearing grinding sound. Second unbalanced blower due to oil deposit one side. Third loose wall clamp vibration. Fourth small or bent duct pipe air pressure noise. We fix with high temperature grease and balancing.

Why Does Suction Become Low?

Suction low when filter more than 70 percent blocked. In auto clean chimney, auto clean only removes 40 percent oil. Remaining stays in blower chamber. Our chemical dip process removes 100 percent oil and suction becomes like new.
