1. Chimney Service - When Should You Get It Done?
For normal cooking 2 times daily, service every 3 to 4 months is mandatory. For heavy frying, tadka, non-veg daily cooking, service every 2 months. Main signs you need service immediately: suction low and smoke not going out, oil dripping from sides and bottom, heavy ghrr ghrr noise, auto clean button not throwing oil in collector, LED light working but motor not starting, chimney vibrating with wall, oil smell coming even when chimney on. Regular service increases motor life from 2 years to 6 years and saves electricity bill by 30 percent because motor does not need extra power to pull blocked air. Our deep cleaning process includes removing both filters, soaking in industrial degreaser chemical for 30 minutes, high pressure water wash at 120 bar, cleaning oil collector box, cleaning blower housing and fan blades manually with brush, checking capacitor value with meter, greasing bearing with high temperature grease rated 220 degree, checking duct blockage with torch light.

2. Chimney Not Working Repair - Why Does Chimney Stop Working?
Main reason is oil choking inside blower housing. Oil becomes hard carbon after heating and blocks air flow completely. Second most common reason is PCB failure due to moisture in kitchen and voltage fluctuation during rainy season. Third is touch panel failure when water goes inside during cleaning with wet cloth. Fourth is motor bearing dry due to continuous heat of 150 degree. Fifth is wiring harness burnt due to loose connection and sparking. Sixth is capacitor weak and value drops from 4MFD to 1.5MFD. Seventh is motor winding burnt due to overload and continuous running for 2 hours without gap. All these problems are 100 percent repairable with genuine parts if diagnosed correctly with multimeter testing. We carry original PCB of all brands, touch sensor, capacitor, motor, blower wheel in our bag so 90 percent cases are repaired in first visit within 45 minutes. No need to take chimney to service center.

3. Chimney Noise Problem - Why Does Chimney Make Loud Noise?
Noise comes from four main reasons. First is dry bearing which makes ghrrr ghrrr sound after 1 year of installation. Second is unbalanced blower wheel due to oil deposit on one side of wheel which creates vibration like washing machine. Third is loose wall clamp which vibrates with wall and makes thak thak sound. Fourth is small or bent duct pipe which creates air pressure noise like whistle when air tries to go out from 4 inch pipe instead of 6 inch. Fifth is motor shaft bent due to wrong handling during previous service. We fix noise by opening motor, cleaning bearing, adding high temperature grease, balancing blower wheel on balancing machine, tightening clamp with rubber anti vibration pad and correcting duct size to 6 inch minimum with smooth bend. After our noise repair, chimney sound goes from 78db to 42db which is almost silent like new brand.

4. Chimney Deep Cleaning - Why Suction Becomes Low?
Suction becomes low when filter is more than 70 percent blocked with oil. In auto clean chimney, many customers think auto clean button means no manual cleaning needed but that is completely wrong information given by salesman. Auto clean button only removes 40 percent oil. Remaining 60 percent stays in blower chamber, oil collector and duct pipe. When we open 1 year old auto clean chimney, we find 2 kg oil inside blower housing. Our process removes 100 percent oil and suction becomes like brand new chimney and smoke goes out in 5 seconds. We use food grade degreaser which is safe for kitchen and does not damage filter coating. We also clean duct pipe which is ignored by other service providers but 80% suction problem is due to duct blockage.
