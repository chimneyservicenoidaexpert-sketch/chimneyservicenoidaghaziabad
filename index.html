Complete Guide: When & Why Chimney Needs Service?

1. Kab Chimney Service Karana Chahiye?

Agar aap daily cooking karte ho to har 3-4 mahine me deep cleaning jaruri hai. Agar aap heavy frying, tadka, non-veg banate ho to har 2 mahine me service karni chahiye. Suction kam lagna, oil tapakna, awaj aana - ye 3 signal hai ki ab service ka time aa gaya hai. Regular service se motor life 5-7 saal tak badh jati hai aur electricity bill kam aata hai kyunki motor ko jor nahi lagana padta.

2. Kyu Chimney Kharab Hoti Hai?

Chimney kharab hone ka sabse bada reason hai oil choking. Filter aur blower ke andar oil jam ho jata hai jo hawa ko rok deta hai. Dusra reason hai PCB me moisture ya voltage fluctuation se card jal jana. Touch panel par haath gila hone se sensor kharab hota hai. Motor me bearing dry hone se jam ho jata hai. Aur sabse common hai duct me mitti ya panchhi ka ghosla ban jana.

3. Chimney Me Awaj Kyu Aata Hai?

Awaj ke 4 main reason: 1) Motor bearing me oil khatam - ghrrr ki awaj. 2) Blower wheel par oil ka wajan ek side jyada - vibration. 3) Clamp loose - body hilna. 4) Duct ka size chhota ya fold - hawa takrana. Hum bearing me high-temp grease, blower balancing aur anti-vibration pad laga kar 100% silent kar dete hai.

4. Suction Low Kyu Hota Hai?

Suction low tab hota hai jab filter 70% se jyada choke ho jaye. Auto-clean wali chimney me bhi har 6 mahine baad manual cleaning jaruri hai kyunki auto-clean sirf 40% oil nikalta hai. Baaki oil blower me chipka rehta hai. Hamari chemical dip process se blower ko nikal kar 2 ghante soak karke 100% oil nikala jata hai.
