When Should You Get Service?

Every 3-4 months normal cooking, every 2 months heavy frying. Signs: low suction, oil dripping, heavy noise.

Why Does Chimney Stop Working?

Oil choking in blower, PCB failure due to moisture, touch sensor water damage, motor bearing dry.

Why Does Chimney Make Noise?

Dry bearing, unbalanced blower, loose clamp, small bent duct. Fixed with high temperature grease.
